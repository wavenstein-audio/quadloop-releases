# QuadLoop privacy policy

Version 1, 27 October 2026

QuadLoop is made by Wavenstein, a sole proprietorship registered with the Dutch
Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 42170664, the Netherlands. This policy
covers QuadLoop on iPad (the app and its AUv3 plug-in) and on macOS and Windows
(the app and the VST3, AU and CLAP plug-ins).

## The short version

QuadLoop collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or what you play to
Wavenstein or anyone else.

## Your audio

QuadLoop records what comes in through your microphone or audio interface into
loops, on your device, so you can play them back. That is the only reason it
asks for microphone access. The audio never leaves your device unless you move
it yourself: by exporting a loop, or by saving a project or song that contains
it, to wherever you choose.

## What is stored on your device

Your loops, songs, settings and MIDI mappings are stored on your device, in
QuadLoop's own folder or inside your host's project when QuadLoop runs as a
plug-in. Deleting QuadLoop, or the project, deletes them.

## Network connections

- **On iPad**, QuadLoop makes no network connections.
- **On macOS and Windows**, QuadLoop asks GitHub once a day whether a newer
  version has been published, so it can tell you. The request carries nothing
  about you or your use of QuadLoop, but like any visit to a website it reaches
  GitHub from your internet address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy
  statement. You can switch the check off in Settings, under SETUP.

## Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test QuadLoop through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports,
and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send, with us. We use them only
to fix and improve QuadLoop. Apple's TestFlight terms describe what Apple itself
collects.

## If you contact us

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you and to
improve QuadLoop, and we do not share them. Ask us at any time to see or delete
what we have.

## Changes

If QuadLoop ever starts handling data differently, this policy changes first,
with a new version number and date.
